# Essential TEER 4 to 5

**Live:** [https://oinp.getnorthpath.com/calculator/essential-teer-4-5](https://oinp.getnorthpath.com/calculator/essential-teer-4-5)

Dedicated calculator for an eligible Ontario **job offer** in essential occupations (**NOC TEER 4 or 5**): personal support work, some food service, and similar roles.

---

## What is different from skilled

- TEER factor is always **0**, so the practical ceiling is about **121 / 130**
- That gap is the published grid, not a calculator error
- Experience is often **cumulative** months in the occupation
- Language floor is often **CLB 4** (confirm on ontario.ca)

Focus levers: hourly wage, Ontario experience, language, and region.
