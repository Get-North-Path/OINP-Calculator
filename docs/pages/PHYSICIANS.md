# Physicians

**Live:** [https://oinp.getnorthpath.com/calculator/physicians](https://oinp.getnorthpath.com/calculator/physicians)

**Nav:** Physicians

The only Workforce Priority pathway with **no job offer** and **no employer**. Self-employed doctors score out of **115**, not 130, because the **15-point hourly wage** factor does not apply.

---

## Who it is for

Physicians who can show:

- An **OHIP billing number** (eligibility, not a scoring factor)
- An eligible **CPSO** certificate of registration
- **CLB 6** in all four language abilities

---

## What the page does

- Locks the calculator to the physician profile (NOC **31102** family physicians as the default occupation context)
- Scores the **nine** factors that still apply
- Explains why 90 / 115 is not “weaker” than a worker at 90 / 130
- Covers Ontario **practice time** (not time in one employer’s job) and **practice address** for region points

---

## Region still matters

| Practice location | Regional points |
| --- | --- |
| Northern Ontario | 15 |
| Eastern, Southwestern, Central outside the GTA | 10 |
| GTA except Toronto | 5 |
| City of Toronto | 0 |

For many physicians this is the largest lever they can still choose.
